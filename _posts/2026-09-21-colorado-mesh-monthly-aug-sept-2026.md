Welcome to the very first **Colorado Mesh Monthly** — a once-a-month digest of
everything happening across the mesh, so you don't have to read every Discord
message to stay in the loop. This kickoff issue reaches back across both August
and September 2026.

Inside this issue:

- **General News** — we crossed over 1,000 Discord members, plus an airtime-etiquette reminder as the network grows.
- **Events & Meetups** — a strong showing at the RMHAM Summer Swapfest (Aug 23), and BARCFest coming up Oct 4.
- **Protocol updates** — Meshtastic's MediumFast rollout in Denver, MeshCore scopes and the I-76 link to Nebraska, and the growing Reticulum RRC hub.
- **Gear & Firmware** — new firmware, a safety bulletin, and board notes from the workbench.
- **Community Spotlight, a Photo of the Month, a Story of the Month, and a giveaway** — react to be entered for a RAKwireless WisBlock starter kit.
